# Master coverage matrix: SVC-PC + EKW QC, 2026-09-14

## Status of this document

**v1, partial by design.** This is the proof artifact the "100% certain nothing slips through"
mandate asked for: every known defect class, cross-referenced against every check that exists or
is being built, with WHAT it catches, WHERE in the pipeline, HOW mechanically, WHY it matters, and
an explicit list of what's still uncovered. It is deliberately NOT claiming the full 63-class
inventory yet  -  that consolidated list is the crisis synthesis doc's deliverable, still in
progress as of this writing. Rather than wait idle for it, this version covers every defect class
this session could confirm directly (the 10 original gap-analysis items, the 6 structural-audit
patterns, the 6 content-audit patterns, the 6 root-cause mechanisms from today's crisis
investigation, and the new findings surfaced today by Session D's manual verification
manifest). It will be merged with a1's full inventory once that lands, not replaced by it.

**Every row is tagged by how its status was established**, because a claim about coverage is a
claim about behaviour and this project's own rules say that needs running the check, not reading
its docstring:

- **VERIFIED (this session)**  -  I ran the check myself against real tasks and confirmed the
  result. Evidence cited.
- **VERIFIED (docstring + corpus numbers)**  -  the script's own docstring cites a real corpus run
  with specific numbers (not "should work"); I read the run's output, not just the claim, where
  practical, but did not re-run it myself this session.
- **REPORTED (peer, unverified by me)**  -  another session claims this is built/working. Cited
  with attribution. Not independently confirmed. Do not upgrade to VERIFIED without actually
  checking.
- **GAP**  -  no check, script, or process catches this today. Stated plainly, not softened.
- **PROCESS GAP**  -  detection may exist, but nothing acts on it (e.g. a real finding never reaches
  the certification decision). Distinct from GAP because the fix is procedural, not a missing
  script.
